Privacy

This service accepts a public LinkedIn profile URL and a target-role brief, uses Apify to retrieve publicly visible profile information, and sends the relevant information to OpenAI to generate the report after payment.

What we process

We process the LinkedIn profile URL, the desired role and career goal, optional location and industry, any achievements, tools or CV text you choose to provide, publicly available profile information returned by Apify, Stripe payment/session identifiers, and the generated report.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not receive or store full card details. Long-form achievements and CV text are not placed in Stripe metadata.

AI processing

Retrieved profile data and the target-role brief are sent to OpenAI to generate the report. The application requests store:false for API responses.

Local storage

Your browser temporarily stores the intake draft and signed target-role brief through checkout, then stores the completed report locally so refreshing the page does not trigger another analysis. The signed brief and draft are removed after a successful report.

Contact

A support/contact address will be added before public launch.
