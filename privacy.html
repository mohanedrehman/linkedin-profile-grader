Privacy notice

Last updated 18 September 2026

Mohaned Rehman, operator of LinkedIn Profile Grader, is the controller for the information used to provide this service. For privacy questions or requests, email muhanedzaeem@gmail.com.

Information we process

We process the LinkedIn profile URL you submit; your desired role, career goal, optional location and industry; any achievements, tools or CV text you choose to provide; publicly visible profile information retrieved from the submitted LinkedIn page; Stripe payment and session identifiers; technical request information needed to operate and secure the service; and the generated report.

Public profile information comes from the LinkedIn URL you direct us to analyse. Please submit your own profile, or a profile you are authorised to analyse, and do not paste special-category or unnecessary sensitive information into the optional fields.

Why we use it

We use the information to take payment, retrieve the requested public profile, generate and deliver the purchased report, prevent misuse, troubleshoot failures and provide refunds or support. Processing needed to deliver the report is performed to fulfil the contract with you. Limited security, fraud-prevention and service-reliability processing is based on our legitimate interests in operating the service safely.

Service providers

Stripe processes payment information; Apify retrieves public profile data; OpenAI generates the structured report; and Vercel hosts the site and server functions. These providers may process information in other countries under their own contractual safeguards and privacy terms. Read their notices at Stripe, Apify, OpenAI and Vercel.

The OpenAI request uses store:false. We do not sell the submitted profile information or use it for advertising.

Browser storage and retention

The intake draft and signed target-role brief are held in this tab's session storage through checkout and are removed after successful report generation or when the tab session ends. The signed brief protects integrity but is not encryption. The completed report is stored in your browser's local storage so a refresh does not spend another analysis attempt; you can remove it by clearing site data.

The application does not maintain a customer-profile database. Stripe retains transaction records and the service providers above may retain limited operational, security and billing records according to their own policies and legal obligations.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, object to or receive a copy of personal information. Contact support to make a request. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Automated analysis

The report is generated automatically as career-marketing guidance. It does not make a legal or similarly significant decision about you, and no employer or recruiter receives it from us.
